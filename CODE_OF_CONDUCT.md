# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ShopWave contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participation should be safe regardless of background, identity, appearance, ability, experience level, nationality, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, or gender identity and expression.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and make space for different experience levels.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully and give it with care.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing another person's information.
- Focus project conversations on what is best for the community and the software.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, threats, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or sustained disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, and any other official ShopWave project space. It also applies when someone is publicly representing the project.

## Reporting

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the repository's [confidential reporting form](https://github.com/thomastran117/E-commerce/security/advisories/new). Begin the report with **Code of Conduct report** so it is not mistaken for a vulnerability disclosure.

If that form is unavailable, contact the maintainer through the contact information on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/thomastran117) to establish a private channel. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and will not knowingly ask a person named in a report to investigate it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity and history, consequences may include:

1. A private correction or warning
2. Temporary limits on participation
3. A temporary ban from project spaces
4. A permanent ban

Retaliation, deliberate harassment, threats, or risks to someone's safety may result in immediate restriction.
